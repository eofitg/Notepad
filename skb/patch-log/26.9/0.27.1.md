## 0.27.1 - Chocolate Factory Improvements, QOL Changes, and more!

### Chocolate Factory Improvements

#### Rabbit Factions
- _Being part of a Faction means you can find new Rabbits that are exclusive to that Faction._
- _Finding these Rabbits are special, as they don't get automatically added to your collection. Instead, they'll drop as an item that you can trade with other players or buy/sell on the Bazaar._
- _Right-clicking on them consumes the item and adds them to your collection permanently._

#### Chocobits
- There are 33,333 Chocobits total and they drop twice as frequently as MYTHIC Rabbits, and they each add 0.2x to the multiplier of your Chocolate Factory! 
  - Note that Chocobits are not stored in your normal Chocolate Rabbit Collection and they do not take up space.
  - ...but there's a catch! You must pay 1M Coins each SkyBlock Year per Chocobit you own, or else it'll be released back into the pool for anyone to potentially get! When a Chocobit gets released, they'll give you two SkyBlock Years worth of Chocolate production that they would've made otherwise as a thanks!
  - _If you already have every DIVINE Chocolate Rabbit, the pity for it will be replaced with pity for Chocobits._

#### Other
- Doubled the drop rates of MYTHIC and DIVINE Rabbits. With this change their Pity amounts have been reduced to 250 and 1,250 respectively.
- Added a new Chocobun Shard that can be fused into with Bunbun and any Crime Family shard. Adds +1 Level Cap to Rabbit Bro, Cousin, and Sis per level.
- Buffed the Rabbit Crew Attribute from 2 ➜ 20 to 5 ➜ 50 Chocolate per second.
- Increased Max Chocolate for certain Chocolate Factory levels
  - Level 3: 4B → 5B 
  - Level 4: 10B → 25B 
  - Level 5: 30B → 50B 
  - Level 6: 60B → 100B
