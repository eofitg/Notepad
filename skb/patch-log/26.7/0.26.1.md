#### Slayers
After continuous testing and feedback on Alpha, we have decided to grant some healing after you kill a slayer boss.
Slayer bosses are currently balanced around you having access to full HP and healing resources.
<br><br>
While this healing effect isn't a full heal, this should let players heal to full before their next fight much easier, and also help dealing with cocooned slayer bosses.
- Healing per second is determined by your Slayer Tier 
  - T1: 40 
  - T2: 80 
  - T3: 120 
  - T4: 160 
  - T5: 200 
- Duration of healing scales with your Slayer Level 
  - Base: 6s 
  - Slayer Level 2: 7s 
  - Slayer Level 4: 8s 
  - Slayer Level 6: 9s 
  - Slayer Level 8: 10s
