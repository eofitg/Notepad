## Aug 6
### 0.27 Bug Fixes

### Healing Revamp Changes
#### Mastiff-Related Changes

_they want to save this armor, but it's still..._
- Extended the duration of Radiant Power Orb and Mana Flux Power Orb from 30s -> 1 minute
- Fixed Mastiff Armor's "heal on hit" set bonus not proccing.
- **Removed the negative Defense and Crit Damage debuff on Mastiff.**
- **You now gain +1 Crit Damage for every 2 Defense.**
  - This naturally caps at +150 Crit Damage as the set still has a 300 Defense cap.
> Designer's Note: The original Mastiff rework intended for the armor to be useful when fighting all tiers of Sven Packmaster. The armor is designed to be tankier than the other alternatives you'd have at that game stage.

#### Other Changes
- Nerfed the "average damage" of Enderman Slayer as follows:
  - T1: 900 → 800 
  - T2: 3,200 → 2,720 
  - T3: 9000 → 7,650 
  - T4: 13,500 → 11,000
- Drain Changes - _still..._
  - Now procs once per second. 
  - Each proc recovers 0.5/1/1.5/2/2.5 Vitality
    - (this should be a 25% buff from previous)
- Nerfed the damage of Wither Miners
  - Regular: 12,960 -> 6,000 
  - Master: 162,000 -> 80,000
- **Reverted the change to obtaining Refrigerate.** 
  - This means Refrigerate I costs 1 Volcanic Rock and 10 North Shards again.
  - (Instead,) you can now obtain up to 5 Volcanic Rocks per wave of Jerry's Workshop event, as each gift pile will feature a Volcanic Rock.
- Having an alive Voidgloom Seraph in the area no longer blocks Enderman spawns in the region.
