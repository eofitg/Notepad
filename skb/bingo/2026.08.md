### Routes

> - remember to enchant your gear once you get enough experience levels for every single enchantment

1. landing, go straight to the hub after place a medium storage along side with your cobblestone minion (c2)

2. sell something like cobblestones&planks to buy rogue sword

3. buy a seed hoe, farming wheats until you unlock barn and get full **junior farming armor**, collect nether wart for collection, sell all the wheats and seeds (a1c5)

4. buy some wooden hoes, buy a glass bottle and a glow-stone dust, artificial bow & 2 stacks of arrows, and **starter fishing armor**(also equipments if you want to, but it's not really necessary) (a2c5)

5. collect all the farming resources u see, kill enough cows to craft **leather armor** and cow minion, then warp to the desert (a2c5)

6. collect farming resources, craft a mystical mushroom soup and **cactus&mushroom armor**, after that go straight to the trapper's den and ***[Kill a Trapper Animal]*** with your bow (a4c19)

7. kill more cows to unlock small backpack or just for minions, get all the easy-to-get **souls on farming island**

8. warp to island and ***[Consume a Mystical Mushroom Soup]***, pickup the cobblestone minion and place your cow minions down, craft silverfish pet if you can

9. back to hub, then buy a random pickaxe and start mining coals and irons until you unlock the new mining island, remember to get blacksmith's contact? (a4c21)

10. buy **gold&chainmail armors** if u want to (a6c21)

11. on mining island (a9c26)
    
    - iron, for **iron armor&miner armor** and 2 spare iron ingots
    
    - gold, just get enough for clever sword then craft it, reforge via abiphone?
    
    - lapis, get 6 enchanted form and upgrade your pickaxe to lapis-material, also for the better enchantment table
    
    - red-stone, or lapis depends on what kind of ore people mining on this island, 15 enchanted form for dwarves mine
    
    - diamond, only for **diamond armor(a9)**
    
    - obsidian, only 1 for collection
    
    - get **souls on mining island**

12. back to hub, kill some zombies to unlock spider's den, then warp there (a9c27)

13. talk to flint buddy, trade promising shovel with 2 iron ingots, mine gravel (a9c28)

14. talk to mob-types guy and grandma wolf, get **souls on the ground**

15. kill spider, then back to mining island to kill certain mobs, including creepers, slimes and skeletons (a9c33)
